Mathematical Foundations

The rigorous formalisms underpinning both neurobiological optimization and system stability.

Langevin Equation

dx/dt = -dΦ(x)/dx + ξ(t)

Models cognitive state evolution where Φ(x) is the potential energy function (energy landscape) and ξ(t) represents stochastic neural noise. Meditation reshapes Φ(x) to deepen desirable attractor wells.

Lyapunov Stability

V(x) > 0, dV/dt ≤ 0

A system is stable if a positive-definite Lyapunov function V(x) decreases along trajectories. LaSalle's invariance principle extends this to negative semi-definite cases, guaranteeing convergence to the largest invariant set.

Hebbian Synaptic Update

Δwij = η · xi · xj

Formalizes "neurons that fire together, wire together." Refined by Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity (STDP) for millisecond-level temporal specificity in synaptic strengthening.

Koopman Operator

U_t g(x) = g(F_t(x))

Linearizes nonlinear brain dynamics in a high-dimensional space of observables. Enables predictive generative modeling of cognitive state evolution without requiring explicit biophysical equations.
